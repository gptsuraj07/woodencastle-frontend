Basic Info

How Categories Work

Parent Category
Main category shown in sidebar.
Example: chopping-boards

Sub Category
Category inside the parent category.
Example: walnut-chopping-boards
teak-wooden-boards

Important:
Products with SAME parent category will come under one common section. Subcategory separates them inside.

Example Structure
Chopping Boards
 ├── Walnut Chopping Boards
 │     ├── Tilted Neck
 │     ├── Rustic Edge
 │
 └── Teak Wooden Boards
       ├── Classic Teak
       ├── Chef Board
Adding Product
  • Add Parent Category first
  • Add matching Sub Category
  • Upload max 4 images
  • Add at least one variant with price
Editing Product
  • Click product card on right side
  • Existing images appear automatically
  • Remove old images using ✕
  • Add new images if needed
  • Click Update Product
